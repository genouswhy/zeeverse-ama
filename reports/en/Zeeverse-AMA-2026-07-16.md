# Zeeverse / FOREST AMA In-Depth Report | July 16, 2026

July 16, 2026 | Community Call | Main speakers Euler and Jackal | [English transcript](../transcription/2026-07-16.txt)

The redesigned combat interface is live, and the debate came down to two practical needs. **People commanding Zees for the first time need to understand the moves and abilities. People who have cleared a dungeon hundreds of times need smooth, reliable auto-battle.** The team acknowledged that the redesign focused on early dungeons and that Umbra and Hollow Trials still needed hotfixes. The medium-term roadmap came into focus. Daily Quests give new players something to come back for, an in-game Zeedex encourages collecting, and Treasure Maps in the third chapter offer a rare reward with a unique effect. [July 14 version 1.15.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-15-0-release-notes)

The call described the **proposed effect of the Legendary ring**. Jackal said the target for the rare treasure reward was around **1 in 5,000** and that the ring would give a **20%** chance to roll the loot table again wherever it applies. On the FOREST side, Euler said the Forest Cloud backend was live. The team wanted to support remixable games with optional token issuance and use its tools to make playable ads for Zeeverse.

## 1. The new combat UI is live, and fixes for Umbra and Hollow Trials come first

Version **1.15.0**, released July 14, redesigned the Battle UI, gave **Zee Inspect** a 3D view and a simpler stats panel, and adjusted dungeon battle speed multipliers. It also updated colors and icons for characters and items and changed quest objective tracking. Euler was interested in how it feels for new players to choose, level up, and command a Zee for the first time. The team had seen early signs of improvement in onboarding, with no quantitative comparison before and after the update shared on the call. [July 14 version 1.15.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-15-0-release-notes)

Euler acknowledged problems in later content and said the team aimed to submit a hotfix build that day. For repeat runs, the team wanted auto-battle to be simple and reliable while preserving the fun of selecting abilities manually and watching the fight. Euler floated the idea of spending 10,000 energy in one click to clear 10,000 dungeon runs as an example of how much waiting veteran players would like to cut. The call made no announcement of an instant-clear feature. **Post-call verification.** Version 1.15.1 on July 23 restored the Hollow Trials wave counter and fixed Cragoth's targeting and some combat issues. [July 23 version 1.15.1 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-15-1-release-notes)

While discussing feedback on the combat redesign, Euler explained why the team wanted to give repeat dungeon runs fewer steps in auto-battle.

> **Euler**
>
> “Obviously at the point where you are in, in your journey of repeating the same dungeon, uh, for the hundred time, and you use auto play, we basically understood that, and we offered a way more like kind of easier, uh, and more simplified experience.”

## 2. The first 15–16 minutes need to teach the rules and get players moving

Euler cited an industry benchmark of about **8 minutes** for the median first session in a mobile game, saying players decide quickly whether to continue. **Finding Your Feet** averaged about **16 minutes** at the time, and players got more freedom to explore once it was complete. The team wanted to give players more freedom earlier and introduce skills, gear, characters, and quests in short steps. Jackal said they had spent the past two or three months refining the first hour. Some veterans still worried that UI work would delay a new Raid Boss. The team said its priority was to clear up confusing steps for newcomers so it could run a larger acquisition campaign later. [June 11 version 1.13.0 release notes, where Finding Your Feet first appeared](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-13-0-release-notes)

Euler said newcomers make up their minds quickly. He then explained why the team wants to give them room to explore early in the tutorial.

> **Euler**
>
> “We give them this freedom, they realize they need guidance, and and what we really try to do right now is to find a good balance between giving them this freedom early enough, but then providing them guidance, uh, which comes not in a form of being hard-locked into quest sequence or something.”

Jackal said players still asked in chat where to find **Nomu** and that he might have changed the relevant dialogue. Euler shared a tester's feedback about **Joran**'s appearance and suggested comparing the current guide with a female Shaman in an A/B test involving potentially hundreds to thousands of players. The call announced no results and no decision to change the character. **Post-call verification.** The August 12 release notes listed a new NPC named **Sirel** who takes over early onboarding. They did not say whether that change resulted from a comparison test. [August 12 version 1.16.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-16-0-release-notes)

## 3. Daily Quests start with a common set, and the in-game Zeedex tracks Zees players actually encounter

**Daily Quests** were planned for the release after the hotfix. They would give players a reason to return alongside the existing daily login rewards. The first version would give everyone simple quests so the team could see whether players explored where the quests directed them. If that worked, the game could assign quests based on what each account can do. Someone who can mine coal might get a coal-mining quest, and an experienced player could get a harder challenge. Euler said the team could eventually track and reward these activities through an **Achievement** system. Making 100,000 buckets came up as a joke. No achievement requirements were announced. **Post-call verification.** Version 1.16.0 on August 12 added four new quests per day. Personalization was still only an idea at the time of the call. [August 12 version 1.16.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-16-0-release-notes)

Euler introduced the Daily Quests test. Jackal added what would need to happen before the team tailored quests to individual accounts.

> **Jackal**
>
> “Initially we're, uh, rolling out the daily quest is like one size fits all. And they will like, uh, start with like very simple simple tasks, but the ultimate goal here obviously, if if it proves to be a success and we can invest more resource into it, would be that these \[…\] quests are tailored to the, uh, user state, right?”

An **in-game Zeedex** was on the near-term roadmap so players could look up Zees and track their collection. Jackal pictured two statuses, **Encountered** and **Owned**. A player would have to encounter the Zee directly for it to count. Seeing someone else's Follower would not qualify. He gave the example of someone with a rare Zee receiving battle invites from people who wanted to encounter it. The qualifying encounters, any requirement to win, and rewards were still to be worked out. The official web Zeedex already lists some Zees. This discussion covered bringing the feature into the game. [Official web Zeedex](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee/zeedex)

## 4. Life skills should make levels, tools, and follower Zees matter

Asked whether passive abilities were already taking effect, the hosts pointed out that **Taka** can help with woodcutting. Life-skill effects for other Zees still depend on a new success formula. Euler used mining as an example. He wanted the level of the ore, the pickaxe, and the accompanying Zee to affect the success rate so players can feel the benefit of leveling up and changing tools. The call gave no formula or weights. [June 11 version 1.13.0 release notes on Taka's woodcutting assistance](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-13-0-release-notes)

## 5. Two campaign chapters remain, with a roughly 1-in-5,000 ring drop and a 20% reroll chance proposed

Jackal said two main quest chapters remained and that the team was tying scenes and mechanics together alongside onboarding work. The next chapter would introduce **ring and necklace slots**, Treasure Maps from dungeons, and digging. Players decipher clues, find the buried treasure, and open a chest. Once a player knows the location, Jackal estimated that a dig would take about **3 to 5 minutes**.

| Figure | What it meant on the call |
| --- | --- |
| **About 1 in 5,000** | The proposed odds of finding the Legendary ring as a treasure reward. Players first need to get a map and dig up the chest. The overall odds per dungeon run also depend on the map's drop rate. |
| **20%** | The ring's chance of triggering an extra roll where it applies. A successful trigger rolls the entire loot table again, which may yield another set of drops. The hosts cited dungeon drops and wild Zee drops and said Genesis Loot Pods were excluded. |
| **Average rises from 5 to 6 rolls** | A hypothetical **Hollow Trials** example. If a payout already rolls five slots and the ring rolls the whole table again when it triggers, a 20% chance adds roughly one expected roll. Jackal still needed to check the expected value for this mode and had yet to decide whether the ring would apply. |
| **About 129 days** | Jackal said he used an LLM to estimate **how long the entire player base might wait for the first ring**, given the player population at the time, an assumed map drop frequency, runs of roughly two minutes in Umbra, and the need to dig up each treasure. He did not share the calculation or actual drop data. |

Rings with strong combat stats were still intended for crafting or enchanting on **Land**. The treasure ring's loot effect gives it a distinct use and leaves room for demand for future combat accessories. The odds, name, and list of eligible drops still awaited an official update.

In describing the proposed treasure ring, Jackal explained what a reroll does in eligible situations and why Hollow Trials still needed its own check.

> **Jackal**
>
> “Like dungeon drops, wild drops, you will have 20 percent chance to roll the whole table again. Uh, so, you know, uh, basically you are getting extra slot, extra drop. In, yeah, in 20 percent, uh, of times. Yeah. Uh, so, uh, not sure, not, I mean we gotta check, uh, the expected values for Hollow Trials.”

**Post-call verification.** The November 2 version 1.18.0 notes confirmed that **The Ship** and **The Wicker** drop Treasure Maps pointing to one of **20** digging spots across the map. The announcement gave no odds for the Legendary ring or list of eligible drops for its effect. [November 2 version 1.18.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-0-release-notes)

## 6. Heist needs the new combat system, and players can already disengage from wild fights

**Heist** still needed UI work, camera setup, and integration with the new combat system. Jackal planned to restore the existing rules first, then propose changes and implement them one by one after player votes. Heist was unlikely to get more development time ahead of onboarding work. The call gave no new date.

Players can already disengage from **wild Zee battles** by running away with their Shaman, which gives them a way out of a mistaken click or a fight they cannot win. It can also trigger while players are fighting wild Zees and gathering at the same time. Euler said the distance was quite short at the time and could be adjusted based on how it feels. The same mechanic could eventually offer an exit from World Boss fights. Several people fighting a World Boss at once and real-time fighting in designated PvP areas were potential extensions the hosts mentioned. Players might get to try an experimental version first. [June 27 version 1.14.0 release notes on fixing wild battles that kept going at excessive distances](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-14-0-release-notes)

## 7. Builder starts integrating Forest Cloud, and FOREST wants to help Zeeverse reach new players

### Simple games can be generated in one pass, and complex features still take iteration

Euler said **Forest Builder** could already generate simple on-chain games or apps from a prompt. He said **Forest Cloud** was live and could host backend logic, multiplayer services, databases, and persistent data. The team had tested uses such as leaderboards. Some complex experiences need several rounds of changes. He estimated the team shipped about two or three public updates a week, many of them small fixes users would scarcely notice. For more control, creators could integrate tokens and settlement through **Forest Playkit** and the Agent Skill. As an example, Euler mentioned an existing investor he had met in Taiwan who used the Agent Skill to make a token-based game. The call gave no generation success rates or load test data. [June 15 Forest Builder announcement](https://forest.inc/news/forest-builder-imagine-describe-deploy-on-chain) [July 3 Forest Playkit announcement](https://forest.inc/news/forest-playkit-infrastructure-behind-zeeverse-economy)

The team also wanted to let creators **publish without issuing a token up front** and add a **Remix** option for adapting games already on the platform. Euler cited feedback from players who wanted to change how Diamond Miner and Gold Miner played as a reason the community might want to make variants. Free Builder Credits came up as one way to let people try it. [May 20 Forest Cloud and Builder roadmap announcement](https://forest.inc/news/forest-protocol-agentic-economy)

### Harness is meant to build up design knowledge across genres

Euler said generating visuals was the first step. Next, the team wanted **Harness** to incorporate knowledge of match-three level design, the decisions that matter in strategy games, and the reasons players return. He compared the idea to Perplexity gathering additional context, and suggested working with experts in different genres so the tool can pick up on game requirements a prompt leaves implicit. Model selection would also take cost and generation quality into account. Euler verbally estimated that FOREST was using about **10%–15%** of internal resources at the time.

Zeeverse also wanted to use Builder to make **Playable Ads** at scale. Short interactive ads would distill parts of the game that represent it well so potential users can try them firsthand. The call discussed production and acquisition ideas and gave no figures for ad output, install rates, acquisition costs, or return visits.

After discussing Builder and Cloud, Euler used playable ads to illustrate how FOREST's tools might help Zeeverse acquire players.

> **Euler**
>
> “User acquisition needs a lot of creatives to work with. Uh, and some of these creatives, some of the best performing creatives in the market are playable ads, which are just generally dumbed down mini game versions that hopefully attract the same crowd that would be in, interested into, uh, full experience.”

## 9. My overall assessment

The team has started to account for **both the first experience and the experience of repeating content over the long term**. The same combat system needs to give new players clear feedback and save time for people who run dungeons every day. A Treasure Map can give story players a first discovery and offer people who know the world a long-term goal. A good test of the redesign would be whether the same dungeon runs smoothly for someone learning it manually and for a veteran using auto-battle.

I'll watch **clear rates and run times in Umbra and Hollow Trials with the new combat system, whether Daily Quests bring people who finish Finding Your Feet back for another session, and where the Legendary ring actually triggers and how much effort treasure hunting takes**. For FOREST, the ability to reliably produce and iterate on playable ads will say more about its value to Zeeverse than one successful demo.
