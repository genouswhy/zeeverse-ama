# Zeeverse AMA In-Depth Recap

November 19, 2026 | Biweekly Community Call | Main speakers: Euler and Jackal, with questions from players | Recording: approximately 54 minutes and 27 seconds

The central question of this call was **how Zeeverse can make it easier for new players to get and use their own Zee while preserving the value of the rare loot veteran players earn**.

Several changes are starting to meet in the middle. The map rework aims to cut down on repeated trips while making shortcuts and trips into resource areas carry a risk of combat. Players can already find eggs for specific Zee while training in the wild. The team wants to make eggs easier to obtain and plans to limit new supply for the on-chain Zee collection. Treasure hunting follows a separate approach. Players will need to earn their own maps, and the legendary rings they dig up can be traded. The team is also waiting for the map rework before it starts running ongoing acquisition campaigns in the Nordics, so it can assess retention and spending on a reasonably stable version of the game.

## 1. The gathering rework pairs better tools with the right follower Zee

[00:31](#t=31)

**Two parts of the system were already in place before the call.** The 1.18.2 release notes, published on November 10, introduced passive skill perks for follower Zee. Most Zee have one perk and gain another when they evolve. Chopping trees, mining, or fishing can yield an extra resource, which also grants skill XP. The notes say the proc chance increases every 10 levels and reaches a maximum of 25% at level 99. They leave open which level is used in that calculation. The same release introduced species-specific Zee eggs. A wild Gongo can drop a Gongo Egg, and bosses can drop eggs as well. Existing Fire, Water, and Grass eggs were converted to eggs for specific Zee of the same element. [1.18.2 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-2-release-notes)

The 1.18.3 notes, published on November 16, added craftable **Mithril, Adamantite, and Oceanite** axes and pickaxes. They require Crafting levels **30, 40, and 50**, respectively. The latter two tiers use new Frost and Coral wood from Umbra bosses. Players can already prepare their tools and materials. The next change is intended to make the differences between tool tiers more noticeable. [1.18.3 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-3-release-notes) · [Stronghold of Umbra game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/pve/stronghold-of-umbra)

**The next update previewed on the call** will rework Fishing, Mining, and Woodcutting. For the purposes of this report, it is referred to as 1.18.4, following the pre-call release of 1.18.3. Jackal said higher-tier tools feel only slightly better in the current build. He used axes to explain the new formula. The better the axe, the more a follower Zee’s Woodcutting perk can amplify its strength and improve the success rate. Once the relevant conditions are met, chopping the **first two tiers of trees** can succeed every time. For other trees, the setup improves the chance of success. Jackal suggested switching to the highest-level follower Zee with a Mining perk when mining. The formula, the thresholds for each tree tier, and the parameters for all three skills are still to come in the release notes. ([00:31–02:27](#t=31)) [Woodcutting game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/skills/woodcutting) · [Mining game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/skills/mining)

Jackal used woodcutting to explain how tools and follower perks change the success rate, then specified which trees could become guaranteed.

> **Jackal · [01:31–01:47](#t=91)**
>
> “Basically, at one point you will be so level that you will always succeed in chopping down a tree. Um, and yeah, uh, though this is only for the first two trees, uh, the rest will still continue to go up to high chances.”

This gives the same Zee a more specific role in different activities. Today, you can switch followers to improve your odds of extra resources. After the rework, you will also want to consider the success rate of the Zee and tool together. How much time that saves on a gathering run will depend on the formula and the restrictions on each resource.

## 2. Auto Travel cuts down on repetitive movement, and the map rework gives route choices consequences

[02:30](#t=150)

Auto Travel is intended to reduce manual movement for all players. A community member suggested making it a mount perk. Jackal pointed out that many new players do not have a Llama and brought up mounts that free players could obtain. The team had also considered Llama rentals. It made no commitment on how players would get access. ([02:30–04:04](#t=150)) [Curve Llama entry in the Zeedex](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee/zeedex#curve-llama)

Jackal described how to set a waypoint, then responded to the suggestion that automatic walking could be a mount perk.

> **Jackal · [02:36–03:11](#t=156)**
>
> “You will be able to set the way points on the map and uh auto walk auto walk there. \[…\] But generally what we wanna do is we wanna remove this friction point for uh for all of the players.”

The same update preview included **Aggression**. Some wild Zee will be able to engage a player’s Zee without being selected first. The team has yet to decide whether the first version will use power or level differences to make wild Zee avoid much stronger players. Jackal expected little change in difficulty on the current map. Some veteran players might even find it easier to train while AFK. Euler described how this would work on the redesigned map. Auto Travel might take a player into a dangerous area, and taking a shortcut or leaving the main road could bring them into contact with aggressive Zee. Those Zee could also guard advanced resources, giving well-prepared players a chance to mine and train their Zee in the same area. The team recalled players already managing both activities manually at the coral mining spot. ([04:04–07:41](#t=244))

**Resources will move from scattered individual nodes into Resource Hubs.** When several players go after the same tree today, a newcomer can look like competition. Euler described a logging area with five or eight trees, where players could see each other and still have resources of their own to gather. The counts were examples to illustrate the layout. The team is building the map with an internal tool that lets them move objects directly. Euler estimated that the preliminary layout and graphics for at least half of the regions were largely in place. The art team still needs to add assets, and the positions of resources and wild Zee will change. Euler considered showing images on the call and decided to save the reveal for later. Version 1.18.0 already added named regions. This round of work redesigns the layout on top of them. ([07:34–11:18](#t=454)) [1.18.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-0-release-notes)

Town Square will be reworked. Campfire restrictions have already been relaxed. Version 1.18.3 lets players light fires in places such as the village. Jackal described one player starting a fire near the Stash and keeping it going with logs so others could cook fish at the same fire and save themselves a trip. ([12:11–13:04](#t=731)) [1.18.3 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-3-release-notes)

## 3. The first world boss tests multiplayer cooperation with a movement mechanic

[11:18](#t=678)

The team showed a work-in-progress model of the first world boss, which is slated to get bigger. The boss was described as Grass-type, with visual elements associated with Radiant Zee. Its name sounded like **Mulcher**, with the official spelling still to be confirmed. Euler wants it to bring players together for one fight on the open map. The team will see whether the first boss works as intended before using the same system to add more. ([11:18–13:37](#t=678), [15:38–15:47](#t=938), [19:30–20:07](#t=1170))

After Euler signed off, Jackal explained the prototype’s main mechanic while getting the raffle ready. **The boss can hit the whole group or deal 300% damage to a single target.** The latter attack may knock the target out in one hit. Players can move their Shaman outside the aggro range to avoid the hit and then rejoin the fight. Jackal added that a strong enough team might still be able to use auto-battle. This was a description of the design on the call. Entry rules, spawn timing, drops, and final values have yet to be published. ([41:33–42:26](#t=2493))

Jackal described movement as a way to handle a heavy hit, while noting that sufficiently strong teams might still be able to fight on auto.

> **Jackal · [42:05–42:23](#t=2525)**
>
> “The world boss will be either it hits all, or it hits one person with 300 percent damage. So, maybe you're gonna get one shot. To avoid this, you can use the aggression aggression range, and you can just run out with your shaman, and re-enter the battle.”

For players, the interesting question is whether the first boss gives people different jobs to do. If several players have to read an attack, pull back at the right time, and then rejoin the fight, the open world gains a shared objective alongside gathering and regular battles. The team has yet to announce which release will include the boss.

## 4. Existing dungeons will offer new challenges after Chapter One

[16:57](#t=1017)

Version 1.18.0 released the third main quest, **Third Time’s the Charm**. Players restore the third totem and fight through two new dungeons, **The Ship** and **The Wicker**. The team is designing the last part of Chapter One and its final battle, which it expects to wrap up the main story on the first island. Jackal is also looking for ways to improve existing quests. The call mentioned a quest involving a small area of the existing map that may have been **Great Tree**. Early ideas for Chapter Two involve an entirely new map and regions. ([16:57–19:34](#t=1017), [38:39–39:09](#t=2319)) [1.18.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-0-release-notes) · [Quest game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/quests)

The planned mode is called **Prestige** and would unlock after Chapter One. It gives players who cleared the old dungeons a reason to return. The team has yet to share the combat rules, reward list, or release date.

After discussing the final fight on the first island, Euler explained who would be able to revisit the dungeons and what would be there.

> **Euler · [18:39–19:00](#t=1119)**
>
> “And once that quest is out and completed, it will also introduce prestige mode. So for the people, um, for the players who have completed all this campaign, they will be able to revisit existing dungeons, and they will find uh a completely new challenges. Um, and the new rewards as well.”

New players already have an updated introduction. Version 1.18.3 lets them choose **Chaka, Taka, or Chomp** as their first Zee at the shrine. After getting Waka’s Mask, they fight Aka with help from Inti, Karu, and Yutu. Their second egg comes from the first wild battle on the shore. Jackal added that before players choose their first Zee, the tutorial lets them see the EVO3 forms of the Taka, Chaka, and Chomp lines in action. The original nine Zee are also getting visual updates. Playable EVO3 forms remain well outside the near-term development scope. When asked about dragons, the team said it had yet to consider a separate Dragon type. A dragon-like Zee could have an existing type such as Fire. The discussion of dual types focused on the complexity of balancing the numbers. ([13:39–16:44](#t=819)) [1.18.3 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-3-release-notes) · [Zeedex](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee/zeedex)

## 5. On-chain Zee supply would be capped, and eggs would still drop after players complete their collection

[20:12](#t=1212)

Players wanted to know why the game would keep giving out Zee while the team restricts on-chain minting. Euler recalled that open minting gave training a tradeable reward, while also rewarding people who leveled Zee in bulk and sold them. He said a level 50 Zee could be bought for about **$5–10** at the time, which might make training one from scratch feel pointless to a new player. He used **$5 in acquisition cost per player** as a hypothetical example of how ad spend relates to potential revenue. The team is concerned that greater demand from acquisition could encourage more bulk farming and selling, eventually making acquisition harder to sustain. ([20:12–22:57](#t=1212))

Euler estimated that **about 25,000 Zee had already been minted**. The team wants to make on-chain Zee a limited-supply collection. The number was an estimate of historical minting given on the call. The market change that can be independently confirmed before the call was the removal of the **Zee** tab from the in-game Marketplace in 1.18.2, with listed Zee returned to their owners. Later in the call, Jackal still described the mint restriction in terms of what would happen “once it goes into effect.” He said existing on-chain Zee could still be trained and sold. Whether the restriction is already in effect, the exact cap, and how it will be enforced on each chain are still to be confirmed in a formal announcement. ([22:58–24:37](#t=1378), [39:09–40:06](#t=2349)) [1.18.2 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-2-release-notes) · [Zeeverse Wallet game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/economy/zeeverse-wallet)

The team wants new players to find more eggs in the wild. The call described **a good chance of finding an egg after fighting around 20–30 wild Zee** as the intended experience. It gave no per-battle drop rate, guaranteed threshold, or final configuration. Euler wants ordinary eggs to be used primarily for training within the game, with items playing a larger role in player trading. The team is also considering more uses for eggs, such as exchanging extras for Spirit. A previous version already awarded Souls of Ruin for sacrificing Zee. This discussion concerned improvements to the rewards from the Umbra altar, such as adding useful resources to the drop table. Exchanging Zee for XP and the specific new rewards are still under discussion. ([24:32–26:52](#t=1472), [39:09–40:44](#t=2349)) [Sacrifice reward changes in 1.7.7](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-7-7-release-notes)

The player asked whether drops would stop once a newcomer had one of every Zee. The reply also considered a possible use for duplicates.

> **Host · [26:22–26:37](#t=1582)**
>
> “They still get new eggs on top of that. Uh, if we don't really have to deal with the supply, we may come, uh, may may introduce easier, um, way more relaxed systems to like burn them for for extra spirit or something.”

The question about bots came back to the same issue. The team said it can identify some bot accounts and that players still in the Discord server can appeal there. Stricter account requirements would also make it harder for ordinary players to get started, while bot operators could keep opening accounts. The team therefore wants to change the incentives to level Zee in bulk, mint them on-chain, and sell them. The share of supply attributed to bots was speculation on the call. ([27:25–29:15](#t=1645))

## 6. Players earn their own treasure maps and can trade the legendary rings

[29:19](#t=1759)

Treasure hunting was already in the game before the call. Starting with 1.18.0, maps dropped by The Ship and The Wicker point to **one of 20 locations** in the world, where players can dig up a chest with a shovel. Version 1.18.3 added **Umbra bosses** as a source of maps. [1.18.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-0-release-notes) · [1.18.3 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-3-release-notes)

Players thought the legendary ring might be untradeable as well. The team clarified that **maps are untradeable and the rings players dig up are tradeable**. If someone could buy a dozen or more maps for the same spot and make repeated digs there, they could bypass the rate at which an individual player earns maps and quickly concentrate the ring supply. The team wants the ring to be rare and worth pursuing, while avoiding a stat increase strong enough to trivialize modes such as Heist. In describing the ring’s value, the team also said it “increases the drop rate by 20%.” The item description or a release announcement would need to confirm which drops are affected and where the effect applies. The call did not give the ring’s own drop rate. ([29:19–31:38](#t=1759))

When the player asked why treasure items could not be sold, the reply used the example of a player digging repeatedly at one spot to explain how the rule applies.

> **Host · [31:03–31:41](#t=1863)**
>
> “If we, if the treasure maps would be tradable, then somebody could just buy like 10 or 20 of the same type and just dig all all of them up in one spot. \[…\] But the output you get out of this is tradable, so.”

This draws a boundary between trading items and playing the content yourself. Players can sell the rare loot they dig up, and they still get the maps by playing. The outcome depends on how much supply the added map sources provide and whether the ring has a use that makes players want to keep treasure hunting.

## 7. Targeted tests come first, with ongoing Nordic campaigns waiting for the map

[31:41](#t=1901)

On the Nordics, Euler said the team is running a small number of targeted campaigns in selected markets. The Nordic rollout will wait until the map is finished and then use **evergreen campaigns** to bring in players each day. Bringing in several thousand people now and changing the map in between would make it difficult to compare the groups. The team is doing performance marketing so it can follow what it gets from each unit of spend. It described the future global launch as a different scale of operation. ([31:41–34:36](#t=1901))

Asked what marketing would follow availability in the Nordics, Euler described the current targeted tests before explaining what he meant by a full launch in that market.

> **Euler · [32:35–32:54](#t=1955)**
>
> “And by full launch, I mean starting to serve evergreen campaigns. That means every day the campaign is active, it just keeps on bringing new people. We're still waiting um to complete the map. Because every time you change something, the data that you have acquired also changes.”

| Market | Progress or objectives discussed on the call |

| Philippines | Advertising mainly supports compatibility testing and performance optimization on lower-spec Android devices. |
| Taiwan | Small tests showed positive signs in advertising. Further expansion would need to account for localization. |
| Mainland China | Responding to a player’s question, Euler described the complexity of releasing there and mentioned working with a local publisher as an example. |
| Nordics | The team wants to reach players whose devices, English proficiency, and ability to spend make it possible to study spending and long-term retention. |

The call gave no Nordic budget, cost recovery, retention figures, or global launch date. ([34:36–37:57](#t=2076))

**Heist** was originally a player-versus-player mode built around attacking and defending vaults. The 1.7.3 notes announced a temporary shutdown for a rework. During the raffle, Jackal said the new version was late in development and that he was testing it. He hoped players would be able to try it soon. The team plans to open a dedicated Discord channel at release for feedback on mechanics and possible changes. The reopening date, revised rules, and rewards have yet to be announced. ([46:31–47:28](#t=2791), [54:05–54:14](#t=3245)) [Heist suspension in the 1.7.3 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-7-3-release-notes) · [Heist game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/pvp/heist-under-construction)

A **fishing boss** is a group activity Jackal would like to see. He recalled that fishing used to provide a route to Energy Potions and wants fish to have more uses in combat and multiplayer activities. Version 1.16.0 removed the ability to craft Energy Potions from cooked fish. His idea was to put new fishing spots and campfires at the map’s edge. Players could fish and cook at the same small gathering spot, and new fish could provide minor effects. A player might occasionally hook a boss, and everyone nearby could fight it together before going back to fishing. One reward idea was a basket that holds **20 raw fish**, extending the time between trips to the Stash. The activity could give fish a recurring role in healing and combat. The team did not place it in a near-term release. ## 9. The closing discussion covers risky health swaps and the trade-offs of combined attacks

[47:36](#t=2856)

During the raffle, Jackal talked about an **Umbra bat Zee** whose name sounded like **Nocti**. It could sacrifice some of its own health to heal an ally for more health than it gives up, and the ally would also receive Bloodthirst. A proposed second ability swaps current health percentages with an enemy, with a provisional **six-turn cooldown**. For example, if the Zee is at 10% and its opponent is at full health, the opponent would end up at 10% after the swap. In auto-battle, a Zee at full health might also swap down to an opponent’s 10%. Bosses would take a heavy hit in place of a direct health swap. Jackal said auto-battle would still be available. Zee with more complex abilities are intended for the high-level content in development. ([47:36–49:45](#t=2856))

Jackal also covered several early designs. A dual-axe tank, a gorgon, and a musk ox were examples of drawing one enemy into a duel. An entangled enemy would only be able to attack the tank. A draft for **battle weather** would give one Zee each to the three basic types, Water, Fire, and Grass. The suggested Water weather would prevent Burn and heal Water Zee at the start of each turn, with **5% HP** given as an example. The Fire discussion mentioned increased Burn damage. Jackal could not recall the Grass effect at the time. ([49:46–52:09](#t=2986))

A player asked whether five Zee could combine into a single big attack. Jackal was personally reluctant. As more Zee are added, managing the combined types and base stats would become difficult. He then floated a Dark Matter Zee that drains its allies’ power and fights alone. If he were to take on that degree of complexity, he would rather explore evolution trees. These were ideas discussed on the call. ([52:24–54:05](#t=3144))

The call ended with a raffle. Jackal said he would talk to Cherry about a livestream to show off the designs, tentatively the following week, with no time set. There would be no regular Community Call the following week. ([45:55–47:14](#t=2755), [48:59–49:09](#t=2939), [54:05–54:24](#t=3245))

## 11. My overall take

I am most optimistic about **Auto Travel removing repetitive inputs while the map makes the remaining choices matter**. If everyday routes are safe, shortcuts carry some risk, and resource areas have enough trees and ore, gathering hubs could become places where players run into each other naturally. If automatic routes often send new players into danger, or several players still compete for a single node after resources are grouped together, the experience will fall short of what these changes are meant to achieve.

The economic decision worth watching is how differently ordinary eggs and legendary rings enter circulation. Easier access to ordinary Zee gives players room to try different perks and lineups. Finding a rare ring still requires earning a map and digging up the chest, and the finished ring can then be sold. Whether a minting cap reduces the incentive to sell Zee in bulk will depend on the rules and how they are enforced. Whether ordinary players are willing to train their own Zee also depends on egg drop rates in the wild, what duplicate eggs are useful for, and the time it takes to train a Zee. On-chain supply tells only part of that story.

I will be watching three things. First, whether chopping the same tree with different tools and follower Zee produces a noticeable change in everyday success rates. Second, whether the first world boss gives players at different progression levels a way to contribute in the same fight, and which encounters Prestige gives them a reason to revisit. Third, whether Nordic acquisition after the map rework provides a continuous view of new players on the same version, from choosing their first Zee and getting a second egg to returning and spending. Those results will show whether the two lines of development come together for the same players.
