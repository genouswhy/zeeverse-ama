# Zeeverse AMA Report | 2026-03-19

March 19, 2026 | Community Call | Main speakers Euler and Jackal | The source has no timecodes, so the full duration is unconfirmed

Zeeverse shared its first numbers on where the tutorial was losing players. **A small acquisition campaign in the Philippines was bringing in about 200–300 users a day. New players reportedly spent an average of 80–90 minutes playing on day two, while some still got lost early on.** The team was testing the removal of the first tutorial battle and adding clearer direction between hatching and the first totem. FOREST reported that V2 and HTML5 support were already out. AI Builder, Seed Vault, and Zeeverse token distribution were each at a different stage.

## I. Small-scale acquisition began in the Philippines, with tutorial changes based on drop-off points

[03:58](#t=238)

Euler said his recent travel had primarily been about maintaining relationships with projects, exchanges, and partners. He announced no new agreements. The game team had started a small campaign in the Philippines over the previous and current weeks. He reported bringing in about **200–300 users per day** and quietly rolling out three Google Play updates that week to test onboarding and address performance. The baseline releases were the March 9 tutorial and UI overhaul and the March 11 hotfix for hatching and softlock issues. The call did not identify separate version numbers or distribution channels for the three smaller updates. [1.8.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-0-release-notes) · [1.8.1 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-1-release-notes)

He noted that players still became lost or confused early, while some who made it past that point played for a long time. The reported **80–90 minutes on day two** was an average playtime. The team did not disclose the measurement window, denominator, or day-two retention. An ongoing A/B test removed the first tutorial battle for one group. The team saw potential for a smoother introduction, with data still coming in. The call did not give a significance result, group sizes, or a decision on which version would ship.

In reviewing the Philippines test, Euler described two experiences in the same funnel. Some newcomers got lost early, while some of those who made it through stayed to play.

> **Euler** · [03:58](#t=238)
>
> “On his second day, on average, he spends around 80~90 minutes, which is very very good and very very promising. Uh, the good, the bad part still is uh a lot of people get lost and confused uh early on.”

Jackal said the team would bring in new players with each round of changes to see how the game felt in practice. Some members of the community were already helping newcomers. This explained why onboarding still needed development time that veteran players wanted spent elsewhere. The team expected a regular flow of real players to create everyday demand for game resources and collectibles. It shared no measured change in asset demand or overall player count.

## II. The first campaign segment restores the village totem, with existing players expected to complete it

[12:21](#t=741)

The goal on the call was to **ship the first segment the following week**. It continues from onboarding into the first dungeon, where players retrieve fragments and use them to restore the village totem. Jackal said the plan was to close both bridges and teleport routes out of the village until that was complete, including for existing players. He estimated that veterans would take about 3 minutes and immediately allowed that it could take a little longer. The first dungeon was described as approximately seven floors. The call showed a work-in-progress animation. The later 1.9.0 notes on April 2 confirmed that a new introductory quest replaced Shaman's Journey and that Grass Dungeon was reworked. They did not list every requirement for existing players. [Subsequent 1.9.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-9-0-release-notes) · [Quest documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/quests)

After Euler handed over the game update, Jackal warned returning players that the first quest would also affect how they leave the village.

> **Jackal** · [12:21](#t=741)
>
> “Both bridges uh leading out from the village and teleports leading out from the village will be initially blocked to everybody. Uh, until you complete the uhuh, first, first quest, first part of the quest. But uh guys, do not worry. For for you, it will take uh maximum of like 3 minutes. Uh, m- maybe a bit long.”

The second and third dungeons are planned to lead to a second totem near the sawmill. Restored totems could teleport players between those locations, changing the daily route for players who regularly visit crafting stations. Euler also described a harder version of the final boss after the ending, with corresponding rewards and unique drops. That would give existing players something to tackle alongside updates aimed at beginners. The launch of the first segment and the high-difficulty finale were separate stages.

## III. The team discussed restoring the energy indicator, renaming Coins, and deepening most GEX pools

[07:43](#t=463)

- **Energy display.** Players wanted the always-visible energy indicator back. The team considered putting it on relevant screens, making it optional, or showing it on the Zee tab to keep the interface clear. Regular dungeons, Umbra, and Forge could eventually have separate energy requirements and recovery times so players could play more than one type of content on the same day. The split was still being explored.
- **Coins to Spirit.** Jackal expected the next update to rename Coins to Spirit and change the icon while keeping the same sources and upgrade uses. The idea was to fit the world better when progress comes from defeating Zee or going through the Hoarder. Spirit is the existing in-game upgrade resource. $VEE is the token used for activities such as GEX. [GEX game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/economy/game-exchange-gex)
- **GEX liquidity.** Euler said that the team had rebalanced the pools and that depth had increased for most tradable items, which could make large buys and sales easier. He gave no figures for individual pools, depth changes, slippage, or the differences between on-chain and off-chain liquidity. The documentation confirms how items and $VEE are paired in liquidity pools. [GEX game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/economy/game-exchange-gex)

The subsequent 1.9.0 notes confirmed the return of the energy timer to the main interface, the renaming of Coins to Spirit, and the renaming of off-chain VEE to Gold. The announcement did not introduce separate energy pools for different activities. [April 2 release notes for 1.9.0](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-9-0-release-notes)

## IV. FOREST V2 and HTML5 uploads were available, with AI Builder still set for a future release

[20:05](#t=1205)

Euler reported that the V2 platform was live with a redesigned interface and a set of fixes. Coin Flip was already available as a template. He said that small projects that had airdropped to friends had attracted people who played, and that the team had seen trading volume. **The call also said that creators could already upload and publish their own HTML5 games.** Another template based on random mechanics was intended for that week. On March 18, FOREST had publicly documented Linear, Logarithmic, and Exponential bonding curves, a concrete example of the additional customization available at launch. [March 18 FOREST announcement on customizable bonding curves](https://forest.inc/news/customizable-bonding-curves-powered-by-forest-protocol)

After the Coin Flip template, Euler discussed HTML5 games developers could already publish and gave a target for the upcoming AI Builder.

> **Euler** · [20:05](#t=1205)
>
> “We also did add uh HTML 5 support, so uh people can build uh and publish their custom games already. And one really cool thing uh coming out, I think early next week. Uh, we we already managed tested, but it's coming next week. As the AI Builder.”

AI Builder had passed internal testing, and Euler gave the start of the following week as a target. The idea was to turn prompts into playable apps within the platform. Connectors could eventually support staking, automatic airdrops to players, and referrals. Euler mentioned seeing more tokens locked and said that each launch used an amount of $FOREST. He invited teams building serious projects to submit them. The call gave no TVL figures or date when AI Builder would become available to all users.

## V. The call described two paths for Seed Vault and Zeeverse token distribution

[23:13](#t=1393)

A listener asked how Seeds would relate to the Zeeverse token and whether distribution would coincide with the global release. Euler thought **the global launch would likely be the marketing milestone**. He said that preparing the TGE would take time and that three months was probably too soon. Describing a desirable launch at 20 to 50 times the existing player base was an expectation. He mentioned specific auctions for Seed holders and a separate airdrop channel for the Zeeverse game community, which would account for most of the distribution and take into account existing Lands and Masks and the ongoing P2A campaign. The call disclosed no dates, allocations, snapshots, or exact percentages. An earlier reward guide separated $FOREST from the proposed $ZEE. [Official Zeeverse rewards guide](https://zeeverse.medium.com/masks-lands-p2a-and-staking-forest-x-zeeverse-reward-guide-07b283dbd666)

The question asked about both SEED and distribution timing. Euler's answer on timing paired a possible launch milestone with the work a token event would take.

> **Euler** · [23:13](#t=1393)
>
> “We'll most likely will be targeting uh the global release, because this is a uh big marketing event. Uh, and also like organizing TG is also no small job, so, uh, I don't think it's gonna happen in 3 months.”

Another player asked whether Masks, Lands, and the Collector Profile would still affect later Seed awards or distribution. Euler explained that the **Collector Profile aggregates assets, progress, and bonuses for combinations** and that later distributions would look at its score. The historical guides document combination badges and a Seed-based route, but the call gave no new allocation table for individual assets. In-game activity eligibility, Collector Profile points, and FOREST Seeds are distinct inputs and do not add up directly to an amount a player can claim. ## Overall assessment

The clearest sign of progress will be whether the early confusion identified in the Philippines test turns into quest changes that work. Dropping the first fight might bring more people through the start. The next thing to see is whether they still get stuck when real combat begins in the first dungeon. Veterans have a concrete measure of improvement as well: once they complete the village quest, are there fewer stretches of road to cover on their way to the sawmill and other common destinations? For FOREST, look separately at organic repeat play in the existing HTML5 projects and at how each Seed Vault round actually admits participants and settles rewards once it is published.
