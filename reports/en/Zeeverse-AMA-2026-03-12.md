# Zeeverse AMA Report | 2026-03-12

March 12, 2026 | Community Call | Hosted by Jackal, with Euler absent | The source has no timecodes, so the full duration is unconfirmed

The new tutorial and UI are live. Where does a player go after hatching their first Zee? Jackal explained **how the village totem leads players into the dungeons and how puzzles, a key, and Mask of Roots unlock the second and third dungeons**. The call also clarified what veteran players could look forward to. The team planned a replayable final battle with a Radiant egg, followed by Great Tree and Forge after the main campaign.

## I. The new UI shipped before broad user acquisition, and the team began addressing feedback

[01:11](#t=71)

Jackal recalled how years of features had crowded the old interface. Version 1.8.0 on March 9 shipped changes to the tutorial, minimap, fonts, Stash, and village layout. The March 11 update to 1.8.1 followed quickly, addressing a tutorial item-pickup softlock, the second Zee failing to join the team after hatching, hatching freezes, and the application failing to respond when out of focus. It added buy and sell totals for multiple items in GEX, while noting that some pools still failed to load. Jackal also said the team had made repairs to Stash. Filtering Crafting by material and remembering the last selected filter were planned refinements. [1.8.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-0-release-notes) · [1.8.1 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-1-release-notes)

Jackal opened by asking whether players had tried the new tutorial, then explained why the UI overhaul came before broad acquisition.

> **Jackal** · [01:11](#t=71)
>
> “The UI has never changed, and obviously with time, uh, with new and varied additions to the game, uh, you know, had become more chaotic. So, that's why we did this large overhaul. Obviously, we miss some marks. So, uh, but we go back and improve, we listen to you guys, improve based on your feedback as well.”

A player reported an issue near a village bridge. Jackal said the team was looking into it, and staff had responded in-game. The host asked Cherry to log another player's report that fried fish appeared to remain in inventory after being eaten in battle. The following day's 1.8.3 notes confirmed Crafting material filters, hiding equipped items from Stash, a fix for GEX pools failing to load, and a fix for combat freezes when feeding fish. It remained unclear whether the missing item deduction reported on the call was related to that freeze. [March 13 release notes for 1.8.3](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-3-release-notes)

The starting area, foliage, and map legibility also received adjustments after the redesign. Jackal relayed Cherry's message that wild Zee had been reset. He gave no details about the reason or scope. The Hatchery had seen smaller changes so far. In a future presentation, players might enter the Spirit Realm during hatching and see models of unreleased Zee. The rough count mentioned on the call did not establish which models would become collectible.

## II. Joran leads players to the totems, with different unlocks for the second and third dungeons

[07:27](#t=447)

At the time, Joran said goodbye when players finished the hatching portion of the tutorial. In the planned first part of the campaign, he would assign a follow-up quest. Players would retrieve totem fragments from a dungeon and restore the village's first totem, opening up conversations with villagers and more quests. A vision of Aka would appear after the restoration to warn players against continuing. Jackal described the second and third dungeons in more detail:

| Stage | Gameplay described on the call |
|---|---|
| Second dungeon | Find an NPC to repair the entrance, then solve a small puzzle. Failure leads to a wild Zee battle. A blacksmith is also needed to craft a key. Clear the dungeon and pick up the fragments for the next step. |
| Third dungeon | The entrance is hidden. Complete the Mask of Roots quest and use the mask to locate it. A villager's keepsake found along the way can be returned for a small reward. |
| Fourth and fifth dungeons onward | Unlock conditions and interactions with the world gradually become more involved. The team wants interiors to be as distinct as Umbra, with different entrances, artwork, and scenes on each floor. |

The idea was to keep early obstacles light and add puzzles once players understand battle, crafting, and the map. The game documentation confirms the spelling of Mask of Roots and its existing Woodcutting requirement. The way the dungeons connect and the exact triggers were preview material. [Quest documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/quests)

After describing the key for dungeon two and the mask for dungeon three, Jackal put those requirements in the context of the campaign's difficulty curve.

> **Jackal** · [07:27](#t=447)
>
> “Generally, uh, the earlier you are in the game, uh, the less like friction we wanna create, and then we like keep on adding that up uh gradually. So, uh, yes, uh, and you can uh figure out from that that in dungeons 4 and 5, you will have to uh start doing a bit more complex things to get access to them.”

## III. The campaign finale is planned as a replayable fight, and Umbra's Dark Matter Zee may arrive first

[08:50](#t=530)

Jackal envisioned a late fight against **Aka's Avatar**, with a Radiant Zee fighting alongside the player. Its egg would become available after the battle. Replays would let players obtain more eggs and collect evolution materials, with the full drop rules still to be set. A separate Dark Matter Zee was planned for Umbra and could arrive before the Radiant Zee. Both were conceived primarily as support units, with some damage capability also discussed. The Dark Matter concept centered on lifesteal and blood magic that costs health to cast. Skill values and official names were still unannounced. [Zee elements and evolution](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee)

In Jackal’s preview, a Radiant Zee fights beside the player in the finale, and beating the encounter gives the player a way to start raising one.

> **Jackal** · [08:50](#t=530)
>
> “After the battle, you will be able to get this radian Z egg, and um, start training it up, and replaying the final battle, you will be able to get more eggs, and that's how you'll be able to evolve your upcoming Radiant Z.”

Veterans asked for a more specific answer than “soon” on the late game. The team's answer was to finish the campaign and onboarding first. The Great Tree travel network and world boss, along with the Forge area and its content, were later priorities with work in progress. The call mentioned approximately **2–3 weeks** between near-term modules and gave differing estimates of the full number of modules. Jackal hoped to share a clearer late-game roadmap with Euler later. The call gave no date for Land gameplay.

A player asked about Land, the Forge and new bosses. Jackal put the Great Tree, a world boss and the Forge after the main campaign. The timing for Land remained open.

> **Jackal** · [18:35](#t=1115)
>
> “I'm not saying soon, I'm saying after uh the main campaign ends. \[…\] The onboarding is the m-, the main focus currently.”

## IV. Q&A priorities include delayed public Land buffs and random turn order at equal speed

[10:32](#t=632)

- **Entering the public Land.** A player asked when they could enter the map transcribed as “Binance.” Jackal thought the entrance should work and said he would look into cases where it did not open. Pools intended to grant temporary buffs were deferred while staff focused on onboarding. Access to the area and the rollout of buffs have separate timelines. The Land documentation provides general context. [Land documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/lands/overview)
- **Actions at equal speed.** Ties in battle speed currently result in random turn order. A player suggested breaking ties among teammates by team position. Jackal saw the point and used his equally fast Matoka and Niska as an example of how shuffling the order affects farming. The call did not settle the proposal. [Zeedex](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee/zeedex)
- **Zooming farther out.** A wider view would demand more from devices. The team prioritized optimization and had no immediate plan for more zoom.
- **Life skills.** Jackal said the controls already supported tapping. The training paths were still at an early stage and mostly used basic materials. He cited potential future Land resources such as Adamantite and Oceanite and suggested that making similar items with higher-tier bars could yield more Crafting experience. The call supplied no multipliers, recipes, or Land release date.
- **New-player data and closing.** The project had just shipped hotfixes and was preparing its first user acquisition campaign. Jackal expected to share actual results and retention later. Three one-month Premium Passes were drawn, and winners could open a ticket to claim their codes. When someone asked to give the prize to a friend, Jackal told them to claim the code first and then pass it on. “Get ready for a million players” was encouragement in the closing remarks. No actual user count was given.

## V. Roadmap as presented on the call

- **Live before the call.** The 1.8.0 tutorial and UI, followed by the first hotfixes in 1.8.1. Feedback on items and dungeons continued.
- **Near-term development.** Crafting material filters, the next onboarding module, the first totem, and the second and third dungeons that follow it.
- **Later development.** The later campaign and replayable Aka Avatar fight, the Radiant and Dark Matter support Zee, and then Great Tree travel and its world boss plus Forge.
- **Queued or being explored.** Public Land buff pools, the hatching scene in the Spirit Realm, team-position tie-breaks at equal speed, and higher-tier life skill training.

## Overall assessment

The biggest point to watch is whether quest prompts explain the unlock rules. In the second dungeon, the player needs to understand “repair the entrance and make a key.” In the third, they need to understand “if you can't see it, find the mask.” If clues are hard to find, the new tutorial's easier start could give way to the same confusion. Equal speeds can still make routine farming less consistent for well-developed teams. The island finale and the new Umbra Zee offer reasons to keep building support options. Future versions will show how each section of the campaign actually plays, what replay difficulties and rewards the final battle gets, and whether a beginner can reach the first totem unaided after their initial hatch.
