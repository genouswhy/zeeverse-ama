# Zeeverse AMA Deep Dive | March 5, 2026

March 5, 2026 · Community Call · Hosted by Jackal · The transcript has no timestamps, and the full duration is unverified

Jackal gave two answers on the same call when players asked when the tutorial would come out. At the start, he hoped to ship the tutorial on Monday, with the option to delay the UI rework. **After receiving an update from the team later in the call, he said the tutorial and UI would be submitted for review together, with a Monday release if everything went smoothly.** There were two longer-term developments for players already past onboarding. The first island's story would connect seven dungeons and end in a boss fight intended as a repeatable challenge for trained teams. Two new support-oriented Zee were also in the works, each with a different approach.

## 1. The tutorial and UI would ship together, and veterans were invited to try the first hour

The call showed screenshots from an internal build. Jackal pointed out new text and dialogue, a rearranged village square and vegetation, the minimap, Stash, and the fog of war that remained on the map. The old Stash had been a frequent source of friction, so players who had already cleared the first round of dungeons would benefit from the UI rework, too. Jackal said the team wanted to avoid a Friday launch to allow time for stability checks and fixes. The update in the second half of the call confirmed a combined release. The team intended to submit on Friday and targeted Monday if it passed review without delays. The March 9 announcement for 1.8.0 later confirmed that **the new tutorial, UI, minimap, and reworked Stash** shipped together. [1.8.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-8-0-release-notes)

Jackal opened by saying the UI might come later. After receiving a message from the team, he updated the plan for submitting the tutorial and UI together.

> **Jackal**
>
> “I actually got a message. \[…\] We will not be splitting the UI update, uh, with the uh tutorial, and uh, looks to, looks that we're submitting it tomorrow, uh, so, if everything goes smoothly, then release Monday.”

Jackal invited veteran players to start a new account after the update and report where the story dragged, where the next step was unclear, and where combat felt uneven. The team planned to bring in new players, track retention and drop-off points, and use the results to change dialogue and tutorial length while continuing work on the later quests. He used “ten times as many players” as a hypothetical to explain changes in supply and demand. Veterans would continue providing late-game resources, and more newcomers would expand demand and supply. The call gave no targets for new players, retention, or paid acquisition. Before the call, 1.7.8 had already made it easier to get started by enabling guest access. [1.7.8 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-7-8-release-notes)

## 2. The main quest uses dungeon loot to repair a village structure, then introduces puzzles and bosses

Jackal said work had begun on the first quest after the tutorial. Players would clear the first dungeon and bring **two items** back to the village to repair an important structure. That would open up more characters and dialogue. Unlocking the second and third dungeons would introduce puzzles, more ways to interact with the world, and the main antagonist. The fourth and fifth dungeons would move the Grasslands story forward, with the final boss coming after the sixth and seventh. The tutorial would offer a small preview of the encounter. This would complete the first Grasslands story arc. Great Tree and Forge would still be separate quests for later, with no new details on either during the call. [Quest game documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/quests)

After showing the tutorial, Jackal previewed the first main quest and explained what would happen when players returned to the village after the first dungeon.

> **Jackal**
>
> “The first quest uh will have you complete the first dungeon. \[…\] Return to the village two two items from the dungeons, and restore uh important thing into the village that will also unlock characters \[…\] and they will want to talk to you.”

This was a development plan at the time of the call. Jackal said the team's internal target was to **deliver the next module about two weeks after the initial release**. The team had mapped out the quest path, and the estimate came with no promise of new content every two weeks. Beyond bringing back the two items, players would be able to see whether the dialogue and new interactions led them naturally to the entrance of the next dungeon.

## 3. The first island's boss would be repeatable, and Radiant and Dark Matter Zee were both considered for support roles

The plan was to let players fight the main quest boss again after the first clear, with difficulty rising as their teams grew stronger. Jackal gave a main quest clear at about level 30 to 40 as an example of why the repeatable fight needed to offer higher-level players a challenge. That was an example in discussion. The recommended level for the first clear and the difficulty tiers for repeat runs were still open. The team sketched out how players might get the two new Zee.

While describing the boss that closes the Grasslands story, Jackal explained why players would have a reason to return after beating it.

> **Jackal**
>
> “One of them coming from um uh the boss fight, because we will be making the boss fight replayable uh post the campaign, and I believe it will be, it will also like scale. \[…\] We're gonna like scale it up, and you're gonna be able to get the eggs.”

| Where to find it | Attribute and role | How to raise it |
|---|---|---|
| Repeatable boss at the end of the main quest | Radiant, leaning toward support | Repeat the boss fight for an egg and collect enough evolution materials of the same attribute. |
| Umbra dungeon | Dark Matter, leaning toward support | The plan was to earn evolution materials by playing Umbra repeatedly. The theme would be bats or vampires, with one idea being to consume some of its own health to use abilities. |

The team said there were already more DPS options and fewer choices for support. Jackal reminded listeners that normal evolution rules required sacrificing two Zee of the same attribute. The game documentation also requires the Zee being evolved and both sacrifice Zee to reach level 50. Getting an egg would be only the first step if the new Zee followed the same path. The health cost, healing, and other support capabilities were still design ideas. The call gave no final abilities, drop rates, or names. [Zee attributes and evolution documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/zee)

## 4. The inventory Q&A focused on bags for specific items and progression through crafting

A player described how quickly the 20-slot inventory filled up when carrying an axe, a fishing rod, a pickaxe, and farming supplies. Jackal said **there were no plans for a separate tool inventory or more slots across the board**. He preferred letting players store items from a category in a dedicated bag, such as a Soul Pouch for certain types of souls or teleport supplies or another bag for seeds. Crafting a bag at level 50 or letting a companion Zee carry a few items were examples. The team had yet to find a good way to reduce the space needed for fishing rods and bait.

A player said an axe, fishing rod, pickaxe, and farming supplies quickly filled the 20-slot backpack and asked whether tools could have a separate bag.

> **Jackal**
>
> “No, uh, there's nothing to be a separate backpack. \[…\] Let's say um a soul pouch, but it can hold like three types of souls. Or uh I'm I'm not say, like, this, these are not confirmed feature.”

When asked about closer ties between in-game items and $VEE staking, Jackal said he had discussed it with Euler and that the Ambassador program might play a role. He framed the idea around convenience and said the team wanted to avoid direct paid advantages such as combat buffs or more inventory slots. Listeners brought up collaborations and co-streams. Jackal said he was open to working together when there was a natural opportunity, with no formal collaboration to announce. He also explained why a guest hosted the Tuesday stream. The team wanted to give other Zeeverse streamers a chance.

A player asked whether in-game items would be tied more closely to $VEE staking. Jackal described the design limits he and Euler had discussed.

> **Jackal**
>
> “I think it was in in relation to the ambassador program. \[…\] The base design theme was like, not pay to win. So call it like uh convenience, uh, rather than you know buffs, uh, or you know another inventory, n, no nothing like that.”

## 6. Roadmap as described on the call

- **Available before the call.** Guest access and improvements to the initial download in 1.7.8.
- **Near-term delivery.** The new tutorial and UI were to be submitted for review together. They went public in 1.8.0 on March 9.
- **In development.** The first island's main quest and its dungeon modules, including puzzles, the antagonist, a repeatable final boss with difficulty tiers, and new Radiant and Dark Matter Zee.
- **Under exploration or awaiting discussion.** Dedicated storage bags, companion Zee carrying items, conveniences through $VEE staking, and collaborations with other communities. The call gave no new dates for Great Tree or Forge.

## Overall assessment

The call moved the tutorial's purpose from “show players which buttons to press” to “show players why they should go to the next place.” If loot from the first dungeon changes the village, players have a chance to understand the restoration quests that follow. For established players, the repeatable fight and new support options are the features they can get involved with after the clear. The release notes can show where each Zee's egg drops, whether repeat runs use the same pool of resources, and what each Zee offers in a support role. The inventory Q&A also made the tradeoff clear. Craftable containers could give life skills another use, and they would save players meaningful trips to the Stash if they held the groups of items people actually carry.
