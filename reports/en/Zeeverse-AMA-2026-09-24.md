# Zeeverse / FOREST AMA Deep Dive | The main campaign adds treasure hunts and Forest Studio opens to creators

September 24, 2026 | Community Call | Main speakers identified by how they addressed each other: Euler and Jackal

The new onboarding was far enough along for the team to keep refining it while adding midgame content. Jackal showed gameplay from The Ship and revisited jewelry and treasure digging in Chapter 3, adding that there were about 20 dig sites in the world. The web Collector Profile would also bring Masks, Land, and collection quests into Shaman Rank. For FOREST, the question was how the product felt to use. Euler said Studio had launched the day before, announced a 1,000 USDT Builder Program, and said Remix would let original creators share in the fees from derivative works. ## 1. Chapter 3 ties together the Metal Totem, two dungeons, and 20 treasure sites

[19:23](#t=1163)

Jackal said Chapter 3 was in active development. He estimated another two to three weeks and preferred to count on at least three because QA would find issues. The quest has players repair the **Metal Totem**, unlock a teleport near the furnace area, and enter **The Ship** and **The Wicker**. He estimated about three bosses across the two dungeons, consistent with the previous call's description of one boss in The Ship and two fighting together in The Wicker. This call showed actual gameplay from The Ship, with ghost Zee visible aboard. The team deliberately saved The Wicker and the story cutscenes for players to discover. [September 10 transcript on the first dungeon preview](../transcription/2026-09-10.txt) · [Call transcript on Chapter 3 and the gameplay preview](../transcription/2026-09-24.txt)

With **Treasure Maps**, players get a map, study the image, and find the matching spot in the world to dig up a chest. The design discussed on the call had around **20 locations**. Maps might come from activities like Stronghold of Umbra, and Jackal also floated rare Zee as a source. He expected Chapter 3 to unlock Ring and Necklace slots and award basic jewelry along the way. He also brought up gemstone resources from Land and Mr. Block chests. The call gave no full list of chest contents or opening date. [Official Stronghold of Umbra gameplay documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/gameplay/pve/stronghold-of-umbra) · [Call transcript on equipment slots and treasure maps](../transcription/2026-09-24.txt)

The two figures given on the call applied to different steps. Jackal said the first legendary ring was designed to drop from a chest at **1 in 5,000 (0.02%)**. The ring itself would give a **20% chance to roll the loot table again**. For example, players might get a second set of rewards after clearing Stronghold of Umbra. The team gave no estimate for how long it would take to dig up the first ring. The suggestion of three days was partly a joke. **After the call**, the November 2 release of 1.18.0 officially named Chapter 3 **Third Time’s the Charm**. The notes listed The Ship and The Wicker as sources of treasure maps and said players with a shovel could dig at one of 20 locations. The November 16 release of 1.18.3 added the Umbra Boss to the list of map sources. Neither set of notes confirmed the ring's drop rate or effect. [Call transcript on the legendary ring design](../transcription/2026-09-24.txt) · [Zeeverse 1.18.0 release notes, published November 2 after the call](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-0-release-notes) · [Zeeverse 1.18.3 release notes, published November 16 after the call](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-18-3-release-notes)

After explaining how to find a dig site from the map, Jackal distinguished the odds of finding the ring from the chance of a reroll while wearing it. His next example was clearing Stronghold of Umbra.

> **Jackal** · [19:23](#t=1163)
>
> “And the treasure chest will include a mega rare ring. At a rate of one to 5000, uh, that will be the, uh, first legendary ring. Uh, and it will increase your, it will give you a 20 percent chance to roll the drop table again.”

## 2. A new homepage would showcase the game, with collections, perks, and trading in the player dashboard

[28:21](#t=1701)

Euler planned to rebuild the homepage and account dashboard. The homepage would become an interactive 3D showcase so Web2 and Web3 users could quickly understand the game. The signed-in app would bring together **Collector Profile**, quests, staking, and claiming. The team had a preview and kept it off the call because the copy was still placeholder text. Another clear direction was bringing **GEX** to the web for direct trading and limit orders through a basic Trading Bot. The official game docs explain how GEX already handles resource trading and liquidity. [Zeeverse GEX documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/economy/game-exchange-gex) · [Call transcript on the website and GEX](../transcription/2026-09-24.txt)

The idea behind Shaman Rank was to convert collections into Shaman Points and grant perks by rank. The call covered the number of Masks, Land, specific combinations, and badges. The team also wanted new players to have a path to Rank I without holding assets. One player asked what would happen to an existing holder with ten Masks. Euler said it would depend on their points, and some ranks might change. He used the example of three well-matched Masks with a badge potentially outperforming five Masks that formed no combination. The team wanted combinations to have value in their own right.

Asked how the change could affect someone who already held ten Masks, Euler said it depended on the score. He used a combination of Masks and badges as an example.

> **Euler** · [28:21](#t=1701)
>
> “Just by holding, let’s say, three specific masks and earning few specific badges means that you will also be able to get more points, and and then it could happen that three right masks are actually more valuable than five masks that don’t essentially give you those to items.”

**The official website announcement on October 13, after the call, set out what shipped.** The first version calculated points from eligible collectibles and Totems in linked wallets and tracked Quest Points separately. Rank I Clay starts at 500 Shaman Points. Rank determines XP and Spirit bonuses, which no longer stack with the old Mask bonuses. The announcement also warned that some existing holders might see their bonuses go down after conversion. At launch, the web Market was listed for later activation. The possibility of reaching the first rank without assets still depended on what sources of points would become available. [Zeeverse website update announcement, published October 13 after the call](https://zeeverse.com/news/zeeverse-website-update-shaman-ranks-collector-profile-more)

The initial release already used creators' own OpenAI accounts. Euler's account on the call is the evidence for the exact rollout of the two September updates. ## 5. The Builder Program offers USDT for finished projects, and Remix aims to pay original creators over time

[40:15](#t=2415)

Euler discussed the **Builder Program** at the start and close of the call. The proposed pool was **1,000 USDT**, first come, first served, with the team reviewing and paying out eligible submissions as they came in. Creators could submit a game or app built with the Harness. It had to be complete, usable, and polished. The program would judge entries independently of market cap, trading volume, or whether the token had graduated from the curve. The reward was meant to help some creators cover ChatGPT or Claude subscriptions. The call gave no individual amounts or actual payment list. [Call transcript on the Builder Program and closing remarks](../transcription/2026-09-24.txt)

Euler returned to the Builder Program before leaving. The team wanted people to test the Harness by submitting finished work and to provide feedback.

> **Euler** · [40:15](#t=2415)
>
> “It don’t have to be necessarily a game, it can also be an app. Just build something fun, just build something nice. Uh, we won’t be like looking into like graduations or volumes or anything. Um, we just need, uh, people to test, come in and test the harness.”

**Remix** was still in development, targeting around the start of the following week. Supported Harness projects would have a Remix option. Later creators could change the theme or features of an existing game and publish with their own token. Euler said the original creator could receive **up to 25%** of the fees from derivative projects. He also suggested the original creator might keep sharing in fees from second- and third-generation remixes and beyond. On September 10 he had used about 5% as an example. Both were ideas spoken before launch. The 25% was the ceiling mentioned on this call. The calculation base, each generation's share, and actual payouts still depended on formal rules. ## 7. My overall take

Whether Chapter 3 becomes a turning point in the midgame depends on what players do after finishing the story once. Will they keep running dungeons, looking for dig sites, and working out which gear to use? Maps that point to recognizable places make exploration more meaningful than a legendary ring appearing on the results screen. The website revamp affects what existing players earn. The biggest thing to understand is why the same account can end up with a higher or lower bonus after moving from direct Mask bonuses to Rank tiers.

FOREST has two outcomes to watch. The Builder Program can surface an early batch of projects that genuinely need ongoing maintenance. Remix depends on projects worth reusing before later creators will be willing to share fees. Whether the original creator can see how often a project was remixed, what fees the shares are based on, and what they actually earned will say more about the mechanism than a percentage cap mentioned on one call.
