# Zeeverse AMA Report | 2026-04-09

April 9, 2026 | Community Call | Main speakers: Euler and Jackal | The transcript has no timestamps. The full running time is unverified.

The first section of the main quest is in players' hands. The question for the game is now **what players will want to do in the world once they leave the tutorial**. Jackal shared progress on consumables found in the wild, common exploration chests and the second section of the main quest. Land holders got an answer about rollout order and a legacy unstaking issue. On the FOREST side, the focus was Seed Vault bidding, eligibility for the old airdrop and the Forest Spin event.

## I. The first Seed Vault had two days left, with close to a tenth of circulating SEED committed by the team's estimate

[00:12](#t=12)

The auction under discussion was a seven-day event with two days left. Euler roughly estimated that **80 million SEED** were circulating in accounts and that the first round was close to consuming a tenth of that. The team saw bids concentrated among large participants. It explained that spending SEED this round would reduce the supply available for future bids, while both prize pools and participant counts would change. The reward per unit of SEED would vary from round to round. Euler said the next pool could be of a similar order of magnitude, with no decision yet. One listener suspected the top bids came from developers. Euler denied that the team was bidding and said he had yet to check the top three accounts. The call did not verify who owned those addresses. [April 1 Seed Vault mechanics](https://forest.inc/news/seed-vaults-the-economy-behind-the-forest-protocol-flywheel)

While discussing how spending SEED in the first round affects later rounds, Euler gave the circulating supply he remembered and his estimate from the live auction.

> **Euler** · [03:26](#t=206)
>
> “Last time we checked, I think there is around 80 million seeds in total in circulation across like all the different accounts. So, we are pretty much seeing almost 10 percent of supply being burned in just first auction.”

The official event history lists **Genesis Auction #1** as running **April 4 at 14:00 to April 11 at 14:00 UTC**, consistent with the two days remaining on April 9. April 4 is the start date. The official record shows a **100,000 $FOREST** prize pool, **99** bidders and **8,254,756.07 SEED** consumed. The top **25** received additional multipliers, up to **2.5×**. These were the settings and settlement figures for that round. [Seed Vault history](https://forest.inc/vault)

## II. The holder airdrop remains a separate plan, and Forest Spin began a seven-day event

[04:07](#t=247)

Some listeners thought joining the Vault meant using the SEED they already had to buy back the rest of the airdrop. Jackal explained that the auction discussed on this call was distinct from the second distribution originally planned for holders. The initial $FOREST claim was about **6.67%** of each eligible holder's original allocation. He said staking the first distribution could earn the corresponding SEED. Future plans also included issuing SEED based on assets held and ecosystem participation. Shares of trading competition and Vault rewards would follow each event's rules. Euler described $FOREST as an additional reward for the Zeeverse community. The game's own token had a separate plan, and the call gave no new snapshot, settlement or launch date. [Official rewards guide](https://zeeverse.medium.com/masks-lands-p2a-and-staking-forest-x-zeeverse-reward-guide-07b283dbd666) · [Legacy SEED claim rules](https://zeeverse.medium.com/forest-airdrop-unlock-the-seed-based-claim-model-4c230a66f55f)

## III. Land gameplay is aimed at global launch, and a third party is handling legacy unstaking

[14:14](#t=854)

A player asked the team to give a clearer answer than “soon” for Land. Euler said it **would not launch before the full game release and was targeted for around the global launch or later**. The team said it had made substantial progress on the Land Generator and related artwork, describing the generator's share as “about 90%.” Assigning actual resources, access and hosting, and further polish remained. Resource nodes such as mining and logging were expected to work similarly to the overworld. Tribal PvP and Tribe Wars were still in development. Land would make a large supply of mid- and late-game resources available, and the team did not want to release it all during soft launch. Euler said about 85% of development resources went to Zeeverse, which was still focused on the early game. Jackal acknowledged the shortage of new content for established players and noted that if onboarding failed to retain players, Land might get too few visitors even if it opened. [Official Land Generator introduction](https://zeeverse.medium.com/zeeverse-worlds-from-words-159920ecb2f3) · [Land documentation](https://whitepaper.zee-verse.com/lands/overview)

A holder asked for a delivery date the team could be held to. Euler separated work on the generator from the timing of opening up its resources in the live game.

> **Euler** · [16:14](#t=974)
>
> “But we can't release the lands before we are releasing the game. And and and and that's because they carry uh most of the resources, and if we do, we'll do it now, then hmm yeah, I mean, it just didn't make sense, uh, at at the moment.”

Late in the call, a player asked again about being unable to unstake Land. The team said DevOps had prepared a reproduction and passed it to the third party responsible for legacy staking, whose name sounds like Walt in the transcript. The call attributed the issue to rarity-score changes that affected the unstaking path for some accounts. The team lacked permission to move assets it did not own on behalf of users. It had shifted rewards to Collector Profile and no longer encouraged staking in the legacy program. The call gave no fix deadline or record of successful unstaking. [Zeeverse staking portal](https://zeeverse.com/app/stake)

## IV. The first main quest is live, and the next one would connect the village directly to the sawmill

[20:08](#t=1208)

The team reviewed the first main quest section it had just delivered. It could finally observe how players entered the overworld after the tutorial. Release 1.9.0 on April 2 confirms the new quests, the Grass Dungeon overhaul and the change to dungeon level selection. The call described completion as generally high. Some new players were losing early fights the team had expected them to win, so balancing and fixes were still needed. No denominator, sample size or loss rate for individual fights was given. [1.9.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-9-0-release-notes)

The second section was in progress, and Jackal shared the layout of the dungeon in the well and early boss assets. The regular dungeon floor selection design was also expected to extend to Umbra. Completing the second chapter would unlock travel between totems, allowing players to go directly from the village to the sawmill. The team expected a small server update first and thought **around the end of April** was realistic for the next quest. Release 1.11.0 on May 11 later confirmed **A Longer Ask** and redesigned early dungeons and progression. The May 13 fixes in 1.11.1 corroborate the name **The Well**. What was shown on the call was still in production. [Later 1.11.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-11-0-release-notes) · [Later 1.11.1 fix notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-11-1-release-notes)

In discussing the next quest, the host used the trip from the village to the sawmill to show how completion would change a familiar route.

> **Host** · [33:54](#t=2034)
>
> “Second part of the main quest is that once you complete that, you will unlock uh ability to teleport between totems. So, the moment we have that, you will be able to travel uh generally from the village to the sawmill, uh, just by using the totem pulse.”

## V. Usable pickups and one-time chests give players a reason to explore away from the main quest

[24:31](#t=1471)

The next server update was to scatter berry bushes, mushrooms and apple trees across the grasslands for consumables usable in low-level encounters. Players would also be able to collect light wood such as pine branches for campfires. Jackal expected an initial batch of around **20 common chests** that players could open as soon as they found them. Rarer chests might involve fights or puzzles and offer items with practical benefits or outfits. The team found that giving players something to pick up when they passed an empty house or side path could encourage exploration. Release 1.10.0 on April 20 shipped wild consumables, light wood and exploration chests that each player could open once. The release notes did not give the total number of common chests or detail higher-tier challenges. [1.10.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-10-0-release-notes)

After describing supplies players could pick up, Jackal moved to exploration rewards and the different interactions he wanted for common and rarer chests.

> **Jackal** · [26:12](#t=1572)
>
> “And these common chests, they will work pretty simple. You you kick them open. Now, uh, what we'll also, we're also working on is rare, epic and legendary chest, which will come with the form of challenges, whether that be defeating a Z, or solving a s-, o-, o-, or solving a small puzzle.”

The initial daily login rewards were a simple seven-day sequence. The call gave a wooden chest, an energy potion and Spirit as examples. The team was also planning a new Zee with a new ability for seven consecutive check-ins, with neither a name nor timing finalized. Release 1.10.0 later confirms Daily Rewards and reworked hatchery animations. It does not identify any particular Zee as a seven-day reward. [1.10.0 release notes](https://zeeverse.com/news/1-10-0-release-notes)

## Overall assessment

The main quest shows players where they need to go. Ordinary chests and consumables give them a reason to go out of their way. The next useful observation is whether new players leave after the dungeon or use consumables they found in the wild to take on a few more fights. Land still has to deliver access, resource collection and activities for tribes. Showing an island in the generator is one step toward that. For FOREST, the official settlement page for each round is the place to check prize pools and SEED spent, since strategies can change from one round to the next.
