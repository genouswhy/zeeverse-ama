## 10. Overall assessment

I would look first at how long it takes newcomers to get through the main quest and whether trained players can easily move on to later content. The two quest updates are worth watching, especially for high-level players hoping to see the World Boss and The Forge before global launch. The available evidence describes the designs and delivery approach. Release dates and playable demos for the quests were unavailable, and the veteran players' requests for holder benefits were still just requests.

On the economy side, **genuine demand behind a trade is more telling than another round of staking rewards**. If off-chain listings make it easier to buy Zee and equipment for a battle setup, production and consumption can turn into regular transactions. Actual trading categories, costs, and what buyers and sellers do will help show whether Premium Pass and trading revenue can support content updates. The precise legacy claim cutoff, the next Ambassador Program rules, and the outcome of the Land bulk sale still require complete contemporary announcements or records.
